A-101 Assess & decompose

One deployable, or six?

Every modernization starts the same way: a single artifact that does everything, that nobody can deploy on a Tuesday. Split it and you buy independence — and pay for it in network calls. Here's the trade, live.

The honest half: splitting adds network latency, eventual consistency, distributed tracing and per-service operations. Cut along business capabilities — bounded contexts — not technical layers, and only where the coupling is actually costing you. A well-run monolith beats a badly-drawn mesh every time.
