I build systems that have to work — and lately, systems nobody has built before.

For **15+ years** at **Tata Consultancy Services** and TRDDC I've turned legacy enterprise estates into cloud-native architectures: COBOL and mainframe workloads migrated to Java/.NET microservices, compliance turned into a service, and GenAI put to work reading and rewriting code it was never trained to understand. Two granted patents came out of that work.

I'm now a **PhD scholar at IIIT Nagpur**, working at the edge of quantum computing, machine learning, and cryptography.

### Research — Dynamic Oracle Adaptation (DOA)

The DOA program asks what happens when a quantum oracle doesn't have to be fixed in advance — trading a little coherence for a lot of flexibility. Three lines of work: **HQ-DOA** (hybrid), **FQ-DOA** (fully coherent), **QDOA** (morphological).
